# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers who want a self-hosted intelligence layer over the YC ecosystem. Secondarily: investors and founders studying the batch landscape, hiring trends, and founder backgrounds. Primary context: desktop browser, research mode, exploring unfamiliar data.

## Product Purpose

Open-source platform that tracks 5,200+ Y Combinator companies via automated data pipelines — scraping the YC directory, extracting entities (funding rounds, founder backgrounds, hiring signals), running optional LLM profiling, and surfacing everything through an analytics dashboard. Free to run, MIT licensed, no vendor lock-in. Success: someone forks it, seeds it in under a minute, and has a usable intelligence tool running locally.

## Brand Personality

Archival. Exacting. Organic.

The tone is a field researcher who has catalogued every specimen — confident without being loud, structured without being cold. Not a startup dashboard. A living archive.

## Anti-references

- Standard SaaS landing pages: centered hero, gradient blobs, three-equal-card feature grid, Inter font, pill navigation, AI purple or blue gradients
- "Terminal hacker" aesthetic: green-on-black, Matrix-adjacent, monospace everywhere
- Warm cream/sand/parchment body backgrounds — the AI default 2025-2026 saturated pattern
- Hero-metric template: giant number, small label, gradient accent circle

## Design Principles

1. **Archive over dashboard** — the landing page should feel like the cover of a research journal, not a SaaS product tour. Depth and precision over interactivity and polish theatre.
2. **Specimen over metric** — each company is a catalogued record with provenance, not a data point in a chart. Structural framing (borders, catalogue numbers, specimen labels) carries the brand.
3. **Structure as surface** — grid lines, border rules, and negative space are active design elements, not just scaffolding. Every visible line should be there intentionally.
4. **Intelligence as craft** — the design should read as hand-assembled: typeset with care, not generated. Cormorant Garamond italic for drama. Libre Baskerville for legibility. JetBrains Mono for precision.
5. **Open as identity** — MIT, forkable, transparent. The design signals "you can have this" not "pay for access."

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text contrast 4.5:1 or better against all backgrounds. Reduced-motion alternative for all scroll-driven animations (typically crossfade or instant). No color-only information encoding.
